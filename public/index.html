    How to play Moonclan Mystery

    What you are doing

    You are Bramblepaw, a Moonclan apprentice. A warrior named Willowfur has been murdered. Find the killer before Day 3 ends. Solve the mystery and you become a warrior, then live the rest of your life in the clan.

    How to play

    • PC: ← → or A D to move, Space / W / ↑ to jump, ↓ or S to catch prey while hunting.
    • Mobile: pick Mobile on the title screen, then use the on-screen arrows.
    • Click cats, dens, evidence, area buttons, and story buttons to interact.
    • Open the Notebook (top right) any time to review your clues.

    Where you are

    • Moonclan camp: Highrock, Leader Den, Nursery, Warrior Den, Elder Den, Medicine Den.
    • After solving the murder: hunting grounds, fighting grounds, borders, the Sunclan border, Gatherings, and the Moonpool open up.
    • Sleeping in the Warrior Den (after you become a warrior) advances time to the next day.

    How to find clues

    • Click Moonclan cats in camp to question them. Only the first 2 cats you talk to will give you a clue, so choose carefully.
    • Open your Notebook to mark cats with Cross or Check, and use the one-time Alibi button on a single cat to learn what they say they were doing the night of the murder.
    • The only physical evidence is a tuft of torn fur, hidden in a bush on the far right of camp. Click the bush to start a search minigame and pick the clump that hides the fur.
    • Collect 3 clues in total (2 from cats + the fur). Once you have them all, the Solve buttons appear so you can accuse the murderer.
    • Choose carefully: a wrong accusation, or running out of days, ends the game.
