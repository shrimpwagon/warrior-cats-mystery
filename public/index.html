    How to play Moonclan Mystery

    What you are doing

    You play as a Moonclan apprentice. A warrior named Willowfur has been murdered. Find the killer before Day 3 ends. Solve the mystery and you become a warrior, then live the rest of your life in the clan.

    How to play

    • PC: ← → or A D to move, Space / W / ↑ to jump, ↓ or S to catch prey while hunting.
    • Mobile: pick Mobile on the title screen, then use the on-screen arrows.
    • Click cats, dens, evidence, area buttons, and story buttons to interact.
    • Open the Notebook (top right) any time to review your clues.

    Where you are

    • Moonclan camp: Highrock, Leader Den, Nursery, Warrior Den, Elder Den, Medicine Den.
    • After solving the murder: hunting grounds, fighting grounds, borders, the Sunclan border, Gatherings, and the Moonpool open up.
    • Sleeping in the Warrior Den (after you become a warrior) advances time to the next day.

    How to find clues

    • Each new game, only 2 random cats will give you a real clue. Pinefoot and Mistclaw are too scared to talk. The murderer just gives an alibi. Other cats speak normally but won't add anything.
    • Clues describe the killer by eye color (gold, sky blue, amber, leaf green, icy blue, copper, emerald). Match the eye color to a warrior in camp.
    • Open your Notebook to mark cats with Cross or Check, and use the one-time Alibi button on a single cat to hear what they say they were doing.
    • The only physical evidence is a tuft of torn fur, hidden in a bush on the far right of camp. Click the bush to start a search minigame and pick the clump that hides the fur.
    • You only accuse when you press Solve. Choose carefully — a wrong accusation, or running out of days, ends the game.

    Building trust with a cat

    • Catch prey at the hunting grounds (↓ / S when a mouse appears) and walk it back to a cat. Click them with prey in your mouth and choose Yes, give prey. Trust goes up by 1.
    • Talking to a cat repeatedly also slowly raises trust (every 6 chats).
    • Most cats cap at 3/3. Whiskerstar caps at 5/5; he also gains trust sometimes when you drop prey on the prey pile (chance gets smaller as his trust climbs).
    • The HUD shows Trust X/Y (in all) — how many Moonclan cats fully trust you out of the whole clan. The number drops if cats die or get exiled.

    Getting a mate

    • Win the tic-tac-toe game at the borders one time to earn a Special Rose.
    • Open your Inventory, click the rose, and choose Yes, hold the rose to carry it in your mouth (you'll see a small pink bloom by your muzzle).
    • Build a cat to 3/3 trust, then click them while holding the rose. A prompt asks if you want to give them the rose. Yes makes them your mate; No keeps the rose for someone else.
    • To stop holding the rose without giving it, open the inventory again and pick Stop holding the rose. It returns to your inventory.
    • Eligible mates: Mistclaw, Ravenstripe, Brindleleaf, Cloudspark, Sorreltail, Birchstep, Hollyfoot, and Princess (the kittypet at the borders). Ashfall, Whiskerstar, Pinefoot, and your own kits are off-limits.
    • Mates show (Mates) instead of trust on click. After you mate, dialogue with that cat changes.

    Having kits

    • Five days after you become mates, the next time you sleep, the game asks if you want to have kits. Pick Have Kits to start a 3-day pregnancy. Pick Not Yet to keep the offer open.
    • After 3 more sleeps, your kits are born. They sleep in the nursery their first day, then spread across camp.
    • You can name each kit yourself (type a prefix, press Enter), end naming early, or skip and let the game pick.
    • Kits become apprentices after 6 days, warriors after 12. Ashstar randomly picks the warrior suffix at promotion.

    Clan relationships (Sunclan, Dawnclan)

    • The relationship ladder runs: extremely angry → aggressive → neutral → peaceful → good friends. Sunclan starts aggressive; Dawnclan starts neutral.
    • To make a clan more peaceful: catch prey, walk it to one of their warriors at the border (Sunclan) or at a Gathering (Dawnclan), and choose Yes, give prey. Every 3 prey gifts bumps the relationship up one rung.
    • To worsen relations: click Fight on one of their warriors. Each fight drops the relationship one rung.
    • Once Sunclan reaches peaceful or good friends, one of their warriors will carry a mouse for you each day. Click that cat to take the prey home.
    • Open the Leader Den and pick Clan relationships to check current status any time.

    Living in the clan

    • The prey pile in the middle of camp holds shared prey. Drop prey on it (and feed the clan) or take from it when hungry. It decays a little each day.
    • Patrols after you become deputy — pick 3 cats and they bring back prey, scares, or scratches.
    • Battle warriors at the fighting grounds to raise your max health. Eat a mouse (↓ / S while holding it) for +1 max health.
    • Gatherings happen every 7 days after you become deputy. Walk over to the gathering scene to attend.
    • Oakwhisker in the elder den has four stories: the other clans, the murder, your origin, and the empty nest beside him. Mosspaw sometimes visits the elder den to treat him.
    • At 40 moons you die of old age. Pick continue as a ghost to keep playing in Starclan. Your mate and kits grieve for 4 days; the next Gathering has a eulogy from Ashstar.
